# Nawah Digital — نواة للتسويق الرقمي

**DEPI Round 5 · Graduation Project · Track: Digital Marketing**

Nawah Digital is a data-driven digital marketing agency for small and medium businesses in Egypt's Delta region. The project covers the full launch of the agency: market research, brand identity, owned channels, an integrated launch campaign, lead tracking and reporting, and a pilot engagement with a real local client.

## Key objectives

- Define positioning, service packages and pricing from research
- Launch brand identity and owned channels (website, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google Business Profile)
- Run a 4-week launch campaign (organic + paid) with tracked KPIs
- Deliver a pilot for one real SME client and report measurable results
